Developing!

There is a constant pressure in modern development that things should be absolutely fancy, with many libraries and frameworks for simple pieces of software. One of the many ways this is manifested is through web-development, with javascript and css that is left bloating the page, difficulting access, violating privacy and consuming more resources, so my goal was to make an extremly simple, yet pretty, blog for me.

Here I will talk about whatever I really want, but my focus will be on my journey through politics, philosophy and tech in general, there is only one "constant" and that is "becoming". So change will be my partner throughout this journey, and I will leave the rest to that.
